# Doctor Guide: Writing a Blog Post (Dr. H. A. Halim)

You can write articles in English, Bengali or both, with photos. No coding needed. Works on a phone or computer.

## One-time login
1. Open your website address and add `/admin/` at the end. Example: `https://yourname.github.io/drh-website/admin/`
2. Choose **Sign in with token** and paste the token your website helper gave you.
3. Keep the token private, like a password. Do not share it on WhatsApp groups.

## Write a new article
1. After login, click **Blog** → **All Posts**.
2. Click **Add post** (or **Add Posts**).
3. Fill in:
   - **Published?** On = visible to everyone. Off = saved as a draft.
   - **Web address word**: English letters and dashes only, no spaces. Example: `kidney-stone-tips`
   - **Date**: today's date
   - **Cover photo**: click and choose a photo from your phone or computer
   - **Title**, **Short summary** and **Article**: in English, Bengali, or both. If you write only one language, visitors still see it.
4. To add a photo inside the article, click the image icon in the article box and choose the photo.
5. Click **Save** (top). Wait 1–2 minutes. Your article is now on the website under **Blog**.

## Edit or hide an article
- Open the post, change the text, click **Save**.
- To hide it, switch **Published?** to Off and Save.
- To delete it, use the delete button on the post, then Save.

## Tips
- Use short paragraphs and simple words. Add a heading with `##` or the heading button.
- Photos: keep each under about 300 KB (reduce the size on your phone first).
- Do **not** publish patient names or photos without written permission.
- Do not promise a cure. Write general health information and say "consult the doctor".
- If login fails, the token may have expired. Ask your website helper for a new one.

## বাংলায় সংক্ষেপে
1. ওয়েবসাইটের ঠিকানার শেষে `/admin/` লিখে খুলুন।
2. **Sign in with token** চাপুন এবং টোকেন বসান (টোকেন গোপন রাখুন)।
3. **Blog → All Posts → Add post** চাপুন।
4. তারিখ, কভার ছবি, ইংরেজি ও/বা বাংলা শিরোনাম, সারসংক্ষেপ এবং লেখা দিন।
5. **Published?** চালু রাখলে লেখা সবাই দেখবে, বন্ধ রাখলে খসড়া থাকবে।
6. **Save** চাপুন। ১–২ মিনিট পরে ওয়েবসাইটে লেখা দেখা যাবে।
7. রোগীর নাম বা ছবি লিখিত অনুমতি ছাড়া প্রকাশ করবেন না।
